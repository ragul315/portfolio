About

I like knowing why a system works, not just that it does.

I'm a software engineer at UST, working on the backend side of generative AI. A big part of my work is responsible AI: PII protection with Microsoft Presidio, and a content-moderation layer built so different content-safety providers can be swapped in (it runs on Azure AI Content Safety today). I also build task-oriented AI agents on Temporal, and right now I'm working on a pipeline that uses Docling to turn large documents into vectors for retrieval.

I spend a lot of time on the failure cases: retries that don't turn into retry storms, circuit breakers, and caching that takes pressure off the database. Outside work I'm digging deeper into RAG and the Model Context Protocol, and I post short explainers about what I learn on LinkedIn.
